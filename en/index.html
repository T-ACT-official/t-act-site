PROFILE

An independent artist from Japan creating music rooted in Chill / Hip-Hop / R&B.

T-ACT is an independent music artist based in Japan, recording thoughts and moments born within reality through Chill, Hip-Hop, R&B, and minimal sound.

Music is not only for success or approval.
While facing the structures and responsibilities of everyday life, it is also a way to leave behind the silence and friction that cannot fully become words.

The lyrics do not over-explain. Rather than describing emotion directly, they value what remains as space and traces, intentionally leaving room for listeners to place their own experiences into the work.

Valuing continuity over spectacle and lived sense over correct answers, T-ACT continues to create and release work independently.
