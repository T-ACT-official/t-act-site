PROFILE

Bearing the weight of reality, and quietly leaving it behind as music.

T-ACT is an independent music artist based in Japan, releasing works rooted in Chill, Hip-Hop, R&B, and minimal, introspective sounds.

Music is not for success or approval.
It exists to leave behind, as a record, the thoughts and moments born within reality.

From a position that faces real structures and responsibilities every day,
T-ACT records even silence and friction as music.

The lyrics do not over-explain. Rather than describing emotion directly,
they value what remains as traces and space.
Room is intentionally left for the listener to lay their own experience into the work.

Valuing continuity over spectacle, and lived sense over correct answers,
T-ACT continues to create and release work from an independent position.
